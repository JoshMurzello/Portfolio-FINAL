3D Printed Camera Storage

A compact storage system for keeping camera accessories visible, reachable, and protected without turning a desk into a parts bin.

Problem

Small gear disappears fast

Batteries, SD cards, caps, cables, and adapters need homes that are obvious during a shoot and easy to reset afterward.

Approach

Modular printed trays

The design favors shallow compartments, fast prints, and dimensions that can be adjusted as the camera kit changes.

Status

Useful shop artifact

This page now documents the intent and direction while the next revision gets photographed with the full studio kit.

What I would improve next

  • Add labeled slots for batteries, media, cable wraps, and lens caps.
  • Use a grid footprint so trays can combine into a larger drawer or shelf system.
  • Test felt, TPU, or rubber feet for better protection and desk grip.
