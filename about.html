What Shapes The Work

Three lanes, one operating system.

Engineering, creativity, and community aren’t side quests. They feed each other.

Engineering that starts with real friction.

I like projects where the problem is concrete and the solution has to earn its place: a manufacturing fixture, a mobility build, a robot, a physical interface, a better tool.

  • Internship experience across EV manufacturing, HVAC R&D, and production optimization.
  • Projects spanning BCI experiments, robotics, product design, and fabrication-driven iteration.
  • A bias toward prototypes that answer the important question fast.

Creative work that sharpens the engineering eye.

Photo and video work trained me to care about pacing, composition, attention, and emotional clarity. That changes how I design products and pages.

  • Murzello Studios grew from hobby work into a real visual system and portfolio.
  • I’m drawn to edits and images that make technical work feel cinematic without faking it.
  • Storytelling is not decoration; it changes whether people stay with the work.

People keep the work honest.

The strongest ideas get better when they’re shared, challenged, and built with others.

  • Student ambassador work taught me how to represent a place and connect with people quickly.
  • Team leadership sharpened how I communicate, adapt, and keep momentum when things get messy.
  • Travel, movement, competition, and new environments tend to reset my perspective in useful ways.
