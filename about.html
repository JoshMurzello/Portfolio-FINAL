The mix of engineering, creativity, and community.

Every chapter at Georgia Tech has been about blending disciplines — pairing rigorous engineering with design, storytelling, and people-first leadership. Toggle through the focus areas that shape how I work.

Engineering that starts with curiosity.

From building an electric skateboard from scratch to experimenting with brain-computer interfaces, I chase projects that demand both analytical thinking and messy prototyping. I learn fastest when I'm pushing components across a workbench and iterating in the real world.

  • Mechanical Engineering major, Computer Science minor @ Georgia Tech
  • Research into EEG-driven input systems and human-computer interaction
  • Internship experience spanning EV hardware, HVAC innovation, and manufacturing

Visual storytelling as a second language.

Photography and film started as a hobby and quickly became a studio. I document the energy of cities, capture portraits with character, and publish edits that feel like micro films. Every creative project feeds back into how I see engineering problems — both are about shaping experiences.

  • Built a growing visual portfolio shooting lifestyle, portrait, and travel stories
  • Lead Murzello Studios, collaborating with local talent on branded shoots
  • Mixing design, typography, and motion into digital experiences (like this site)

Community keeps everything grounded.

The best projects happen with people. At Tech, that means guiding campus tours as a Student Ambassador, captaining a nationally competitive Bollywood fusion dance team, and saying yes to new experiences — from pickleball tournaments to spontaneous road trips.

  • GT Student Ambassador: connecting students, alumni, and future Yellow Jackets
  • Captain of a nationally ranked Bollywood fusion dance team
