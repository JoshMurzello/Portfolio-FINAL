The breakdown

Four tasks. One autonomous robot.

This class involved students being tasked with creating a fully autonomous robot using combinations of mechatronic components including actuators, pneumatics, DC motors, potentiometers, solenoids, and more, in conjunction with an Arduino device.

This year's theme was "Barbenheimer" which was incorporated in the objectives of the class. 4 primary tasks were assigned to students:

  1. 01 / Rescue

    Rescue Ken

    Rescue Ken from the center of the track.

  2. 02 / Clear the path

    Knock down the fences

    Knock down the fences in the way to rescuing Ken.

  3. 03 / Sort & return

    Sort the isotopes

    Sort the different colored isotopes and return them to the starting position.

  4. 04 / Reach the rig

    Raise the casing

    Raise the explosive casing to the top of the test rig which is elevated 5 feet in the air and rotating.

Completion summary

How we finished.

In the end, out of 68 teams, we placed 12th.

However, some notable accomplishments that we feel better represent our teams experience include:

  • Achieving 95 points (second highest of the every round of the competition)
  • Coming into the elimination bracket ranked first of 32 teams
  • Utilizing a unique cascading lift mechanism
  • Scoring higher points consistently over any of the top 4 ranking teams
